Your choice

With your permission, this site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see how the playground is used. Until you do, Google's script is not loaded and no cookie is set. Who runs this site

osc2r2 and this site are a free, non-commercial project run by me, okchan08 on GitHub. My profile there lists ways to contact me. Questions about this page or application can also be raised in the site’s issues.

What is measured, if you allow it

Google Analytics records the pages you visit, details your browser reports (device type, browser, language and the page you came from), and an approximate location that Google derives from your IP address. In the playground it also records:

  • for each run: whether it was started with the button or from a link, which bundled sample and map it used (or whether you used your own), its timestep, time limit and seed, how it ended, and how long it took;
  • whether copying a share link worked;
  • whether a file you opened was a map or a scenario.

Never sent: the scenario you write, a map or file you open, file names, any output or messages from a run or the checker, and the part of the URL after #, which is where a share link carries the scenario. The playground runs entirely in your browser; nothing you write is uploaded.

Cookies and storage

If you allow Analytics, Google sets the cookies _ga and _ga_PQZ953P2WJ, which distinguish your visits from other people’s and last up to two years. Your choice itself is kept in your browser's local storage under osc2r2-analytics, so that you are not asked on every page. Declining after allowing deletes those cookies.

Why, and on what basis

To learn which parts of osc2r2 are used and where runs go wrong, so that this project can be better. The legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw above at any time; withdrawing consent does not affect data collected before then.

Who receives it, and for how long

Google, which provides Analytics, receives the data and may process it outside your country, including in the United States; Google participates in the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework How Google uses this data. Advertising features are turned off. Analytics retains the event data for two months; I see only the aggregated reports Analytics produces, which don’t let me identify you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — in the EU, the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, for example — you may have the right to ask what data about you is processed, to have it corrected or erased, to object to its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. Since I cannot identify you in Analytics' reports, the quickest way to have your data removed is to decline above, which deletes the cookies that link your visits together.
